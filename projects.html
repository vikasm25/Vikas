Python · Data tool

NetCDF Generator

Jun 2025

A desktop tool that turns tabular data into structured netCDF datasets for climate and environmental modelling.

NetCDF Generator is a Python-based GUI application that simplifies the conversion of tabular data (CSV/Excel) into structured NetCDF4 datasets with full multi-dimensional support. Built on the Xarray backend, it enables researchers, scientists, and engineers to prepare climate, environmental, or geospatial data for advanced modelling and analysis without complex coding. Features include custom grid resolution, NaN/zero handling, and light/dark theme switching.

Research · Air quality

Source Apportionment of Atmospheric Pollutants

Dec 2022

Analysing pollution sources and seasonal patterns in Sangareddy, Telangana.

Carried out a study using 2019 ambient air quality data to identify and characterise atmospheric pollutant sources with the U.S. EPA Positive Matrix Factorization (PMF) model. Investigated seasonal variation, computed air mass back trajectories with HYSPLIT, and performed Potential Source Contribution Function (PSCF) analysis. Python was used for data preparation and R for plotting and visualisation.

Research · GIS

Cyclone Titli and Land Cover Change

Jun 2022

A geospatial assessment of land use and land cover in Brahmapur, Odisha, before and after the cyclone.

Conducted a geospatial analysis to assess the impact of Cyclone Titli on land use and land cover in Brahmapur as part of coursework. Used Sentinel-2 satellite imagery and the Semi-Automatic Classification Plugin in QGIS to create false colour composites, perform supervised classification, and map changes before and after the cyclone. The project demonstrated how remote sensing and GIS can support disaster impact assessment.

Research · Water

Municipal Water Supply Efficiencies in India

Jun 2022

A comparative study of urban water utilities across Chhattisgarh, Gujarat, Maharashtra, and Telangana.

Evaluated the operational efficiency of 441 urban water utilities across four Indian states using Data Envelopment Analysis (DEA). Identified top-performing utilities through scorecard analysis and assessed input-output efficiency. Findings suggested that utilities in Maharashtra and Gujarat performed better than those in Chhattisgarh and Telangana, with substantial theoretical potential for cost and staffing optimisation. The study highlighted the need for sustainable resource management.
