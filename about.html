Researching Persistent Pollutants in the Deep Ocean

I'm Vikas Kumar Meena, a Ph.D. research scholar in environmental engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Hyderabad. I use environmental models and data to study the fate and transport of pollutants in the ocean.

Research focus

Deep-Ocean Pollution Research

My work examines the fate and transport of persistent pollutants, especially PCBs and mercury. I use global environmental and ocean models to study long-range transport and ocean partitioning, alongside field sampling to understand atmospheric processes.
